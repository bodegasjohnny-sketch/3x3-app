# 3x3 · Reel Studio (test build)

Aligned with **ARCHITECTURE_v2.md v2.8** and research pack v1.2 (`/workspace/video-app-pack`).

Front end for Johnny Bodegas's video-automation app. **Static, simulated, noindex.** Custom domain: **https://3x3.johnnybodegas.com** (GitHub Pages certificate approved 2026-10-22, expires 2027-01-20; Enforce HTTPS on; DNS: GoDaddy CNAME `3x3` → `bodegasjohnny-sketch.github.io`).

> TEST MODE: no real jobs, keys or posts. All data lives in this browser's localStorage. Use **Clear data** in the top banner to wipe it.

> **The browser checks are advisory, not the guarantee.** The script placeholder gate, banned-word lint, disclaimer exemption, topic filter, brand-mismatch warning and voice-length window all run in this browser and can be bypassed with devtools or by clever text. The real guarantee is the **3x3 server re-running every check on the saved text (script lines, CTA, topic, rules, approvals and file hashes) before it accepts an approval and again before anything is published**. Hardening in the browser stops here; new bypasses should be fixed in the server checks.

Every change autosaves.
  - Goal step: a preset for each goal, plus monetization warnings (TikTok Rewards needs videos over 1 min; mass-produced, templated videos get demonetized; YPP thresholds).
  - Voiceover can be turned off. When it's off, ElevenLabs isn't required.
  - Brand step: live 9:16 preview with the 250px top / 400px bottom safe zones and a WCAG contrast check. White text on the highlight colour is blocked, and pill text automatically switches to dark.
  - Affiliate goal: a disclosure line is inserted automatically into the caption previews, end card, rules and final output.
  - Step 8: **mandatory AI disclosure**, mapped per platform (YouTube "Altered or synthetic content", Meta "AI info", TikTok "AI-generated content"). It can't be skipped, the on-screen tag is locked on for photoreal styles, and Continue/Lock are blocked until it's acknowledged. No-claims rules are always on for health, finance and crypto. The disclaimer is locked on for high-risk niches (health, finance, crypto, kids). Made-for-kids toggle.
  - **Forced rule bundles** (rule set **v1.2** from `taxonomy.json` `rule_bundles`: health, finance, crypto, alcohol, true_crime, kids) lock their toggles: the bundle disclaimer (NFA for finance/crypto, alcohol and true-crime disclaimers), no-claims rules, the bundle's banned words (they can't be removed), and made-for-kids with YouTube-only publishing (kids). Bundles with `goals_blocked` (crypto, alcohol) block affiliate/e-commerce goals; Lock is disabled with an explanation.
  - **Custom topic** ("Write my own topic") is chosen before Lock, checked against the banned words (whole-word match) and screened with word-boundary keyword lists that pull in the matching bundle (e.g. "100x guaranteed crypto airdrop" triggers crypto + finance; "beginner", "engine", "drum" don't trigger alcohol). It's read-only after Lock.
  - Alcohol bundle (liquor history, or an alcohol custom topic), per **architecture v2.8** §7: never auto-published, scheduling off, and **3x3 never makes alcohol public**. For every post on every platform you answer **age-restricted** or **18+ isn't available: keep private / don't post**; each answer simulates the server call `confirm_age_gate(publish_id, platform, file_hash, text_version, outcome)` (text_version `agt_2026_10_v1`, the M4 seed of `app_flags.age_gate_text_version`) with outcome `age_restricted` or `kept_private` (final). YouTube: uploaded private; set 18+ in YouTube Studio; 3x3 leaves it private; visibility is your decision. TikTok: uploaded to your TikTok inbox as a draft; set Audience controls 18+ first, then choose the visibility yourself and post it in the app; keeping it private means leaving the draft unposted or deleting it (3x3 never direct-posts alcohol and never tells you to make a post public). Facebook: you first check the Page's own age restriction (a required tick), then answer; only then does the server publish with `targeting.age_min` (18, or 21 for the US and for markets with a drinking age of 19 or 20, from the new Target market field) and `is_ai_generated=true`. Instagram: confirm the account minimum age, then the server publishes. Keep private on Facebook or Instagram means nothing is sent. Alcohol and true-crime projects are never made for kids. No affiliate/retail links; the alcohol disclaimer is forced. All simulated: nothing is uploaded.
  - TikTok (all projects): until TikTok's audit passes (`app_flags.tiktok_direct_post_audited`, simulated by a Test tools toggle, default off), every TikTok post goes to your inbox drafts. After the audit, non-alcohol, non-kids posts may direct-post with the privacy you pick from a dropdown ("Who can view", no default; Queue is blocked until you choose).
  - Johnny's decisions: a **$20/day spend cap** per user (default and maximum; shown on Review and the Project page). The test build **enforces it with a simulated daily ledger**: once estimated spend would pass the cap, the stage stops at "Needs you" (a burst of 40 jobs stops at $20). TikTok 30–60s is allowed for reach and marked "Reach only: not eligible for Creator Rewards" (61s+ for Rewards). Kids is YouTube-only with made-for-kids forced. Crypto keeps the strict bundle.
  - Review: every missing connection links straight to its fix, a per-video cost estimate (labelled as an estimate), a compiled rules JSON preview, export/import of the intake as JSON, and a required **compliance check** before Lock. **Import JSON is validated** (enums, hex colours, numbers, lengths), re-applies the forced bundles and the cap, and always clears the AI-disclosure and compliance acknowledgements so you must tick them again.
  - Locking creates **intake v1 (read-only)**. "Edit as v2" starts a new version; earlier versions stay viewable and can't be changed.
- **Projects / Project page**: pipeline Rules › Script › Voiceover › Stills › Video › Final stitch › Publish. Gates follow MASTER_PACK: **Script** (edit any line before approval; placeholder template lines must be rewritten; banned words block it. The locked disclaimer is exempt only as whole standalone sentences: a disclaimer sentence must start the line or follow . ! ? and a space, and must end with . ! ? or at the end of the line, so 'Crypto is high risk-free', 'Crypto is high risk free money' and 'Not financial advice, just buy' are still checked. A second pass removes disclaimer sentences anywhere (one at a time and all together) and can only add hits, which catches splices like 'Crypto is high riskNot financial advice-free'. Bidi controls are stripped and RLO runs reversed before the lint, the topic filter and banned-word checks, so a reversed 'Guaranteed returns' is caught. If you ban 'financial advice' yourself, your ban wins over ordinary lines: 'This is not financial advice.' is flagged, because only the locked disclaimer's own sentences are exempt and the disclaimer is already on screen), **Voice** (play/redo per line), **Storyboard** (stills), **Master** (final, with the clip preview inside it; Video has no gate of its own). Final shows −14 LUFS / ≤ −1 dBTP / length as **targets**, not measured values. AI labels follow `ai_disclosure.label` **auto|always** (auto: on for realistic styles or a realistic synthetic narrator; step 8 shows why). The default voice follows the market (PH → a PH voice) until you pick one. Publish warns unless a connected channel/Page name is an exact normalised match for the project brand or handle (case, spacing, punctuation, '(Page)', '@', fullwidth and zero-width characters ignored; lookalike letters, combining marks such as 'Mo̵ney' and bidi/RTL override characters count as a mismatch, and the card shows the name with the direction controls removed plus a note). **Production must compare the channel/Page ID saved with the brand, not names.** The Voice gate checks the VO length against the spec window (target ±3 s, e.g. 42–48 s for 45 s): outside it, Approve is blocked unless you tick a logged override for that exact take (an override on an in-window take is not applied and the log says it isn't needed), and Final shows the actual VO length next to the target.
  - **Script placeholder gate (advisory; the server re-checks):** a hook or beat line is a placeholder when (0) it contains a bidi embedding, override or isolate (U+202A–202E, U+2066–2069): refused outright, on the CTA too, under its own 'hidden text-direction characters' heading (not the placeholder one). Bare direction marks (LRM U+200E, RLM U+200F, ALM U+061C) don't reorder Latin text and come with ordinary pastes, so they're silently stripped on save (the edit log notes it) and before every check; (1) it is lorem-ipsum filler, or has too few real words: **3 for the hook, 2 for every other beat line**. Real words are letter words of 2+ characters that aren't English stop-words, lorem or keyboard mash (Tagalog words such as 'ka' count; 'ng'/'mga' count despite having no vowel), plus numbers and amounts (₱500 and 20% count as one word each) and all-consonant acronyms of 2–6 letters written in CAPS (BNPL, SSS, TV, BTC). A leading beat label like '#5:' doesn't count, and at least one letter word is required. A line with one real word plus two or more stop-words counts as 2, so 'Sleep on it.' passes as a beat line. Checked at this commit, these pass as beat lines: 'Wait a day.', 'Shop with a list.', 'Use cash.', 'Set a budget.', 'Delete the app.', 'Save ₱500 a week.', 'Put 20% aside.', 'Mag-ipon ka.', 'Save more.', 'Try it today.', 'Sleep on it.', 'Avoid BNPL.', 'Check SSS.', 'Skip TV.'; '.', emoji, 'x', 'Spend.', 'Do it.', 'No.' and lorem ipsum stay blocked. The hook still needs 3 real words. (2) it still matches *any* template variant (all drafts, all beats), as typed or character-reversed: more than half of the template's word pairs survive (the topic written in full counts as the topic slot), or at least 2/3 of its words are present while the line adds at most 2 words of its own. Normalisation: HTML entities, NFKC, zero-width/format characters stripped, the Unicode confusables table pinned to v18.0.0 by URL and sha256 (`js/confusables.js`, built by `tools/build_confusables.py`) plus Latin letters with a stroke/bar (ø, ɵ, ł, đ…), Cherokee Ꮻ and regional indicators (🇴 → o), Greek/Cyrillic maps, 1/I/l treated alike. Leet is folded only inside a word: `$ @ ! |` at the start or end of a word are separators ('one!' reads 'one'), symbols and digits between a word's first and last letter are mapped (p01nt → point), and digits at a word's edge are split off as a number ('point1' → 'point' + '1') unless the fully mapped word is a template, topic or lorem word ('on3' → 'one'). Known limits (it's a word-overlap check): synonym swaps; a template cut to half its word pairs; template words padded with 3 or more *different* filler words, interleaved or appended after shuffling (one or two distinct fillers are caught); a space inside a word ('ve rifiable') isn't rejoined. Decided from the saved text on every render, save and Approve, never from a flag. Earlier drafts' edit diffs are kept through Regenerate. Status chips: Not started / Running / Needs you / Awaiting approval / Done, plus Failed (with Retry) and Re-approval needed.
  - **Hash-locked approvals**: each stage stores the hash of its inputs (the rules hash plus upstream output hashes), and each approval stores input + output hashes. Swapping a still or regenerating anything upstream voids the approvals after it ("Re-approval needed"); those stages must be regenerated before continuing.
  - Swap any single still; Download (gives you a manifest JSON for now; on an alcohol project Publish stays not done until you answer each platform); Queue for publish (needs the platform connections).
  - Test tools: make the next job fail, 3× speed, reset the simulation, delete the project.
- **Connections**: *Worker (your Grok Bot)*: paste the routine webhook URL and routine key (test mode accepts only fake `test_` values and rejects real-looking keys; only the last 4 characters are kept), then a fake signing secret is shown once to save in your bot as `THREEX3_SIGNING_SECRET`, then "Send a test job" (with a simulated failure path) and rotate links. *Bot-stored secrets* (`XAI_API_KEY`, `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`): "Stored in your Grok Bot, not here", with a simulated status toggle and a "How to add it in your bot" note; xAI API is the only image/video route in 3x3. *Connect where you publish*: YouTube, Facebook Page, Instagram, TikTok via a simulated official OAuth popup in your own browser, showing "Connected as {account} (simulated)" / Needs sign-in again / Disconnected. Your bot never holds social logins. (Google Drive is not part of the architecture and was removed.)

A test-tools toggle on Connections shows it and cycles reasons.

## What is simulated vs real
| Real | Simulated |
|---|---|
| Intake logic, research taxonomy/styles (pack v1.2), rules compilation, requirement and warning computation, input validation/escaping, hashing, versioning, localStorage persistence, JSON export/import | All jobs. `js/simulator.js` stands in for the user's Grok Bot worker: script/stills/clips/final are placeholders. Worker test job, signing secret (fake, generated locally), OAuth popups, connection statuses. Spend ledger (estimates). Download returns a manifest instead of an MP4. Publishing never posts anything. |

**Secrets:** 3x3 holds no API keys. xAI and ElevenLabs keys and `THREEX3_SIGNING_SECRET` live in each user's Grok Bot. The routine key field is simulated (only fake `test_` values; nothing real-looking is ever stored). **Planned for production (not built yet):** job signing: 3x3's server signs each job sent to the routine with an Ed25519 key that never leaves the server, the routine checks it against a public-key list signed by two offline 3x3 root keys (a primary and a backup) built into the template, and the per-user signing secret signs only the routine's replies back to 3x3; routine key and OAuth tokens encrypted server-side, and **approvals/hash locks, rule-bundle enforcement and the spend-cap ledger all enforced server-side**. In this test build they run in the browser and can be tampered with via devtools, which is acceptable only because nothing real happens. No analytics, no external requests.

## Architecture / swap points
- `js/adapter.js`: **the data adapter (swap point)**. `LocalAdapter` uses localStorage today. Implement `SupabaseAdapter` with the same interface: `projects` and `jobs` tables, Realtime for `subscribe()`, and a server function that (planned) signs each dispatch with the app's Ed25519 key and POSTs it to the user's routine webhook; callbacks are signed with the per-user signing secret. Secrets stay in each user's Grok Bot secret store. The schema is in the file header.
- `js/simulator.js`: local fake worker; delete it when going live.
- `js/model.js`: connections, stages, requirements/warnings, secret guard, hashing, AI-disclosure map.
- `js/intake.js`: the intake (from the mockup). `js/pipeline.js`: stage/job state machine. `js/pages.js`: Projects, Project, Connections, Onboarding. `js/app.js`: shell/router.
- Style IDs follow `styles.json` (the collage style is `editorial_collage`). Studio and brand names are kept out of style names and prompts.
- `tools/shoot.py`: Playwright end-to-end click-through plus screenshots (`shots/`). `tools/qa_checks.py`: regression checks for every QA pass (XSS, import, custom topic, alcohol flow, spend cap, script placeholder gate bypasses and genuine rewrites, disclaimer exemption, brand match, axe, focus, mobile banner; 228 checks at this commit); results in `shots/qa_checks.json`.

## Known gaps
- No backend, no auth, no real worker. State is per-browser.
- The 3x3 bot template link isn't public yet (it's a placeholder in Onboarding).
- Logo/frame-overlay upload and "Custom style from reference images" are visual placeholders from the mockup. Voice previews use the browser's speech engine (labelled as a sample, not the ElevenLabs voice) or say unavailable.
- Script and still content are placeholders. The cost estimate uses unit counts only (no live pricing).
- Custom topics are normalised before screening (HTML entities and %-encoding decoded, NFKC, Cyrillic/Greek lookalikes including capitals plus the full Unicode confusables table (`js/confusables.js`), leetspeak with 1/I/l treated alike, spaced or dotted letters, plurals; banned words are also matched after splitting at hyphens, so 'cure' catches 'cure-all'; bare 'doge' and 10x/100x/1000x flag as crypto unless a clear non-crypto context such as Doge's Palace/Venice or zoom/lens/camera/microscope is present; common non-alcohol phrases like 'high spirits' and the whole names 'Gin Blossoms', 'Rum Tum Tugger' and 'Ale Ale' are ignored; bare 'sol' counts as crypto only when written as the ticker SOL/$SOL or next to a crypto or price word such as buy, price, staking, coin or token, so mixed-case 'Sol LeWitt' and 'History of Sol' stay clean, but the ticker rule is case-sensitive, so ALL-CAPS titles ('HISTORY OF SOL', 'SOL LEWITT RETROSPECTIVE', 'SOL Republic headphones') and a price word next to sol ('Sol price of tomatoes') still flag crypto. Those false positives are kept on purpose because they err on the strict side. A title-case 'Sol' with no context is no longer flagged; 'Old Gin Mill' is deliberately still flagged as alcohol, since a gin mill is a bar) and matched against per-bundle term lists, but it's still a keyword list, not a classifier; the production rules step should re-check topics server-side.
- `.pyc` files from `tools/__pycache__/` are still in the git history of commit 9ecdbc4 (removed from the tree since face9fd and 404 on the site). Removing them from history needs a history rewrite and force-push, which we're not doing.
- true_crime `subject_rules`/`pii` and alcohol `visual_rules` are carried in the compiled rules for the worker, but there is no vision QA in this build.
